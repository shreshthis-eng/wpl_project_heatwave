Severe Heatwave Alert Bulletin for India

National Heatwave Alert

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued a Severe Heatwave Alert for several parts of India. Extremely high temperatures are expected across the northern, central, and western regions of the country over the next few days.

National Weather Highlightss

  • Maximum temperatures may range from 42oC to 47oC in affected areas.
  • Hot and dry winds are expected during the afternoon.
  • Heatwave conditions are likely to persist for the next 3–5 days.

General Safety Advisory

  1. Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water.
  2. Avoid direct sunlight between 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM.
  3. Wear lightweight, loose-fitting cotton clothing.
