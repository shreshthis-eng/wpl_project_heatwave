Data Sources and Stakeholders

How We Make Weather Predictions and Send Alerts (Step by Step):

  1. We collect real-time weather data from Automatic Weather Stations (AWS).
  2. We recover the stored weather records from the IMD database.
  3. We collect and study the satellite images and radar updates about the weather and climate of regions.
  4. Then we analyze temperature change, humidity, wind speed, rainfall patterns in the area.
  5. After studying the data and analysis we detect the possibility of Heat Wave (HW) or Severe Heat Wave (SHW).
  6. Thus weather forecasts are generated and safety precautions are given.
  7. Eventually Alerts and advisory notices are released on the website.
  8. Further the conditions are monitored continuously by experts and updates are given.

Stakeholders: Their Roles and Actions:-

  1. Meteorological Department

    • Collect and validate weather observations.
    • Generate weather forecasts.
    • Issue heat wave and rainfall alerts.
    • Share reports with government agencies.

  2. Disaster Management Authority

    • Monitor emergency weather alerts.
    • Coordinate rescue and relief operations.
    • Activate emergency response teams.

  3. Local Government

    • Open emergency shelters.
    • Arrange drinking water facilities during heat waves.
    • Coordinate with healthcare departments.

  4. Healthcare Department

    • Prepare hospitals for heat-related illnesses.
    • Conduct public health awareness campaigns.
    • Deploy medical teams.
