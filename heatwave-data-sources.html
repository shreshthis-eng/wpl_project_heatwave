How We Make Weather Predictions and Send Alerts

1

Collect Real-Time Weather Data

Data is collected from Automatic Weather Stations (AWS).

2

Retrieve Historical Records

Previous weather records are obtained from the IMD database.

3

Satellite and Radar Analysis

Satellite images and radar updates are studied to understand regional weather conditions.

4

Analyze Weather Conditions

Temperature, humidity, wind speed and rainfall patterns are analyzed.

5

Detect Heatwave Conditions

The collected information is used to identify possible Heat Wave (HW) or Severe Heat Wave (SHW).

6

Generate Forecast

Weather forecasts and suitable safety precautions are prepared.

7

Release Alerts

Heatwave alerts and advisory notices are displayed for the public.

8

Continuous Monitoring

Experts continue monitoring weather conditions and provide updated information.

Stakeholders: Their Roles and Actions

🌦️  Meteorological Department

  • Collect and validate weather observations.
  • Generate weather forecasts.
  • Issue heatwave and rainfall alerts.
  • Share reports with government agencies.

🚨  Disaster Management Authority

  • Monitor emergency weather alerts.
  • Coordinate rescue and relief operations.
  • Activate emergency response teams.

🏙️  Local Government

  • Open emergency shelters.
  • Arrange drinking water facilities.
  • Coordinate with healthcare departments.

🏥  Healthcare Department

  • Prepare hospitals for heat-related illnesses.
  • Conduct public health awareness campaigns.
  • Deploy medical teams when required.
