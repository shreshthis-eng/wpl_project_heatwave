What is a Heatwave?

Understanding extreme heat events and their impact on India

A Heat Wave is a period of abnormally high temperatures, more than the normal maximum temperature that occurs during the summer season in the North-Western parts of India. Heat Waves typically occur between March and June, and in some rare cases extend till July.

IMD Criteria for Heatwaves

  • Heat Wave not considered till max temp reaches at least 40°C for Plains, 30°C for Hills.
  • Normal max temp ≤40°C: Departure 5-6°C = Heat Wave; 7°C+ = Severe Heat Wave.
  • Normal max temp >40°C: Departure 4-5°C = Heat Wave; 6°C+ = Severe Heat Wave.
  • When actual max temp is 45°C or more, heat wave should be declared.
🏥

Health Impacts

  • Heat Cramps: Edema and Syncope with fever below 39°C.
  • Heat Exhaustion: Fatigue, dizziness, nausea, muscle cramps, sweating.
  • Heat Stroke: Body temp ≥40°C with delirium or coma — potentially fatal.
🌍

Causes of Increasing Heatwaves

  • Climate Change: Rising global temperatures lead to more intense heat events.
  • Early Summer Onset: Higher temperatures arriving earlier in the year.
  • Weak Pre-Monsoon: Reduced rainfall limits natural cooling.
  • Urban Heat Island: Cities trap heat due to concrete infrastructure.

Safety Measures During a Heatwave

Important guidelines to protect yourself and your community

💧

Hydration & Diet

  • Drink water all day long, even if not thirsty.
  • Drink ORS, lemon water, or fruit juices.
  • Avoid alcohol, tea, coffee, and very sugary drinks.
  • Eat light meals with watermelon and cucumber.
☀️

Outdoor Safety

  • Avoid going out between 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM.
  • Wear a hat, sunglasses, and use an umbrella.
  • Put on sunscreen with high SPF before going outside.
  • Do not do heavy work outside during hottest hours.
🏠

Indoor Safety

  • Stay in a cool place with air conditioning or a fan.
  • Keep windows and curtains closed during the day.
  • Take cool showers or baths to lower body temperature.
  • Use light-colored, loose-fitting breathable clothing.
❤️

Health Monitoring

  • Check on elderly family members and neighbors.
  • Know the signs of heat exhaustion and heatstroke.
  • If dizzy, weak, or nauseous — move to cooler place and drink water.

🚑 In Case of a Heat-Related Emergency

Contact your local emergency services or healthcare provider immediately.

How we collect, analyze, and distribute heatwave information

📋 How We Make Weather Predictions & Send Alerts

  1. We collect real-time weather data from Automatic Weather Stations (AWS).
  2. We retrieve stored weather records from the IMD database.
  3. We collect and study satellite images and radar updates.
  4. We analyze temperature change, humidity, wind speed, and rainfall patterns.
  5. After studying the data, we detect the possibility of Heat Wave (HW) or Severe Heat Wave (SHW).
  6. Weather forecasts are generated and safety precautions are provided.
  7. Alerts and advisory notices are released on the website.
  8. Conditions are monitored continuously by experts and updates are given.
