About the Heatwave Intelligence System

Message from the Disaster Management Officer:

"As temperatures continue to rise, it is our collective responsibility to stay informed, alert and prepared. Heatwaves can have serious effects on public health and day to day life. We advise all citizens to stay hydrated, wear light clothing, avoid prolonged outdoor activities during peak afternoon hours, and regularly follow official weather updates. Special care should be given to children, senior citizens, outdoor workers, and also individuals with existing medical conditions. By staying alert and following official advisories, we can reduce the impact of extreme heat and protect the health and well-being of our communities."

- Chief Meteorologist


Our Objective:

The Heatwave Intelligence System is designed to monitor weather conditions, predict heatwaves, and provide timely early warning alerts to the public. It helps meteorologists, disaster management authorities, and government agencies make informed decisions while enabling citizens to take necessary precautions. By delivering accurate and reliable weather information, the system aims to reduce the impact of extreme heat and improve public safety.


Data Sources:

The Heatwave Intelligence System collects weather data from multiple reliable sources, including the India Meteorological Department (IMD), satellite observations, weather sensors, Automatic Weather Stations (AWS), and environmental monitoring systems. This data is continuously analyzed to provide accurate forecasts, detect heatwave conditions, and issue timely alerts.


Stakeholders:

The Heatwave Intelligence System supports a wide range of stakeholders, including citizens, meteorologists, disaster management authorities, government agencies, healthcare organizations, and emergency response teams. These stakeholders use the system to monitor heatwave conditions, plan preventive measures, coordinate emergency responses, and ensure the safety and well-being of communities during extreme weather events.
