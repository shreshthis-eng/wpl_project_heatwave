Important Safety Measures: are some important safety measures to follow during a heatwave

    HYDRATION AND DIET:
  • Drink water all day long, even if you do not feel thirsty.
    •
  • Drink oral rehydration solutions (ORS), lemon water, or fruit juices.
    •
  • Avoid alcohol, tea, coffee, and very sugary drinks.
    •
  • Eat light meals with fresh fruits and vegetables like watermelon and cucumber
    •
    OUTDOOR SAFETY:
  • Stay out of the sun and avoid going out between 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM.
    •
  • Wear a hat, sunglasses, and use an umbrella or a damp cloth on your head.
    •
  • Put on sunscreen with a high SPF before going outside.
    •
  • Do not do heavy work or hard exercise outside during the hottest part of the day.
    •
    INDOOR SAFETY:
  • Stay in a cool place, preferably with air conditioning or a fan.
    •  K. J. Somaiya School of Engineering, Mumbai-77 (Somaiya Vidyavihar University) Department of Computer Engineering
  • Keep windows and curtains closed during the day to block out heat.
    •
  • Take cool showers or baths to lower your body temperature.
    •
  • Use light-colored, loose-fitting clothing made of breathable fabrics.
    •
    HEALTH MONITORING:
  • Check on elderly family members, neighbors, and those with health conditions.
    •
  • Be aware of the signs of heat-related illnesses such as heat exhaustion and heatstroke.
    •
  • If you feel dizzy, weak, or nauseous, move to a cooler place and drink water.
    •

In case of a heat-related emergency, contact your local emergency services or healthcare provider immediately.
